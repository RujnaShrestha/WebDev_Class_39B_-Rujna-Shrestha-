Introduction

Mr. Mike Cruz is the Director and Co-Founder of the company, with over 10 years of experience in product development and brand strategy within the beauty and skincare industry. Known for his ingredient-first, science-backed approach to formulation, he has helped build the brand's reputation for clean, effective skincare. He holds a degree in Cosmetology and is passionate about clean, effective formulations. His research focuses on skin-loving compounds such as Hyaluronic Acid (C14H21NO11), Salicylic Acid (C7H6O3), Azelaic Acid (C9H16O4) and minerals like Zinc 2+oxide,studying how these ingredients can improve product safety and skin health. Under his leadership, the brand has grown a loyal, ingredient-conscious customer base. Outside of work, he frequently says, "good skin starts with good chemistry."
